Pokémon Survivors: a free Vampire Survivors-style Pokémon game

Pokémon Survivors is a free, unofficial fan game that mixes Pokémon with Vampire Survivors. It is an action roguelite (bullet heaven) that runs in your browser and on your phone, with no download and no sign-up.

How to play

Pick your starter Pokémon, walk around the map and let it attack on its own. Survive 15 minutes of wild Pokémon hordes, collect experience, learn moves, equip held items and evolve mid-run. At the end, face Mewtwo. Then keep going in endless mode until Arceus comes hunting.

Features

  • 27 starters from every region, Kanto to Paldea (Charmander, Squirtle, Bulbasaur, Piplup, Froakie, Fuecoco, Sprigatito and more), each with its full evolution line.
  • 23 moves and 19 held items: max out a move with the right item and it evolves (Ember + Charcoal becomes Flamethrower).
  • Type matchups, burn, poison and paralysis, plus a special attack per type such as Blast Burn, Hydro Cannon and Frenzy Plant.
  • 171 wild Pokémon from all 9 generations in 5 tiers: every run draws a different cast, from basic Pokémon early to evolved ones late.
  • Legendary bosses: Onix, Snorlax, Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Mew and Mewtwo.
  • Five biomes per run (Route 1, Viridian Forest, Mt. Moon, Mt. Silver and Lavender Tower) and weather that shifts type matchups: harsh sunlight, rain, sandstorm, snow and fog.
  • Enemies with personality: Chansey heals the horde, Bronzong shields it, Nidoqueen calls in reinforcements, Voltorb explodes, Magnemite fires beams and Hitmonlee leaps over the horde.
  • Legendary rifts: step into a walled arena and face Lugia, Ho-Oh, Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza or Dialga for rare rewards.
  • Endless mode after Mewtwo: the horde never stops and Arceus, the reaper, hunts you from 20:00.
  • Wild shinies and shiny starters, Poké Dollars and permanent upgrades in the Poké Mart.
  • Map events: buff altars, healing fountains, locked chests guarded by an ambush, traps that hurt the horde too and a fleeing Meowth full of coins.
  • Pokémon Mystery Dungeon-style pixel art, in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
  • Works on phones with a touch joystick and installs as an app.

FAQ

Is Pokémon Survivors free?

Yes. It is free, with no ads and no purchases. The code is open source on GitHub.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs right in the browser, on PC or on your phone.

Is it an official Pokémon game?

No. It is an unofficial, non-commercial fan game, not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, GAME FREAK or Creatures. Made by Pedro Barretto.
